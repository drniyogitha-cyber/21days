Frequently asked
Who is this challenge actually for?

Anyone with one goal, habit or task they keep postponing — no prior coaching experience needed. If you know what you need to do but don't follow through, this is built for you.

Will I form a permanent habit in 21 days?

That's not the promise here. 21 days is long enough to experience real consistent action and learn what helps or gets in your way — not a guarantee of a lifelong habit.

What exactly do I get for ₹500?

The opening webinar, daily exercises and action plans for all 21 days, WhatsApp community support, 3 weekly review meets, and the NLP-based approach used throughout — everything in the "What will you get" section above.

What if I miss a day?

You pick back up the next day. The weekly review meets exist to help you notice what got in the way and adjust — the point is consistent action over 21 days, not a perfect streak.

Can I choose any goal, or does it have to be work-related?

Any single goal, habit or task you want — exercise, reading, a business goal, a personal project. The only rule is choosing one and staying with it for the 21 days.
